Know Your Rights

The Constitution guarantees your rights. The Municipal Systems Act empowers you. When the government fails to act, you have legal recourse.

Right to Basic Services

Section 27 of the Constitution guarantees the right of access to sufficient water. Section 24 guarantees a healthy environment. If Emfuleni fails to provide water or sanitation, they are violating your constitutional rights.

Municipal Bylaws

Emfuleni Local Municipality has bylaws regarding noise control, public nuisances, building regulations, and waste management. You have the right to demand enforcement of these bylaws.

What To Do When Govt Fails

1. Log a formal complaint with the Municipality.
2. Escalate to the Municipal Manager.
3. Contact the Public Protector if there is maladministration.
4. Approach the Courts for a mandamus order (forcing them to act).
5. Join ActionSA to hold them accountable politically.
